Study Overview

This study investigates cognitive and behavioral processes across multiple experimental stages. Participation involves five structured parts completed online over several sessions.

Study Parts

  • Part 1: Consent and demographic questionnaire
  • Part 2: Behavioral task (approx. 20 minutes)
  • Part 3: Questionnaire on perception and decision-making
  • Part 4: Follow-up task after a delay
  • Part 5: Final questionnaire and debriefing

Who Are We?

We are a research group from the Department of Psychology investigating cognitive mechanisms underlying perception, learning, and decision-making.

Learn more about our department: Department of Psychology

About the Research

This research is grounded in contemporary cognitive theory, combining models of information processing, learning, and behavioral adaptation. By examining responses across multiple stages, we aim to better understand how individuals integrate feedback over time.
